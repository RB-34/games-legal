Beadwood – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Beadwood (“the game”). The game is developed by Karolína Rutová, Czech Republic (“I”, “me”), who is the data controller. Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

What the game itself stores

The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress and settings (levels, stars, sound, language and similar) are stored only on your device. I never receive them. You can delete them in Settings → “Reset progress”, or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may process data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → “Ad consent”.
  • On iOS, the game asks for permission to track. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • Without consent, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use limited data for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Legal basis

Personalized advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalized advertising needed to provide the game free of charge is based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. As I do not hold any data that identifies you, requests about ad data are best directed to Google. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
